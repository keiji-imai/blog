My high school music taste was pretentious as hell. I had convinced myself that any song with lyrics was categorically bad; putting words into my brain made it harder for me to think for myself and distracted me from studying. I only listened to three playlists: [classical](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/4EnWawaGZZ83bR6U5vUb4D?si=9b36acd201ba42e3), [jazz](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/6cjB2XFf7Nnoo7OwQOSy6C?si=bbf77b0c2a80428a), and [EDM](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/3g0x0zFtnL6Zm94nUaxwOj?si=b166aa2669e44460)[^1]. And I basically judged everyone for listening to anything else.

My pop-hating musical worldview started falling apart when I overheard some jazz musicians discussing the latest Taylor Swift album. Why the hell were *good musicians* diving into the artistic nuances of *bad music*? I began to wonder if maybe getting attached to a few niche music genres and assuming everything else was bad wasn't the right way to appreciate music.

When I went to college I decided to diversify my taste by asking people about their favorite artists as a way to connect with people I meet. This was more of a success than I was expecting! Most people like talking about their favorite artists and a lot of them listen to [*really interesting stuff*](https://open.spotify.com/track/6FX6S0Vl6tjRCTnWVqVdto?si=09891dc4386a40a8). Rather than judging people I explored [crazy music](https://open.spotify.com/track/29VdNzgJIxzlIDkIYjFQqS?si=71d165d1662e4d2d) which I had never seriously listened to before.

 I found out that Eminem wasn't [the only good rapper](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/1lCELGBSNdOT51jky1ZKEj?si=c8d9e6ec74db4f15). My friends introduced me to [Japanese](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/4o5W1QZf3GQbR6LFGbxSlU?si=952542236da84a27) and [Spanish](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/61hWh5WJ4GM20f7Gp67jDM?si=c3473ebf7a22462d) music. Sometimes white girl music [kinda goes hard](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/7B6Z60M9Hbu7CABumKyWY8?si=d41fba6a5b3b4630). I love [melodic house](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/4PE3kyHTtWUiab2zao9DDv?si=0273cede1fab4215) and the [blues](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/01ajIslpxVEd0EMTr2W6yi?si=376bc3ce7ff841a4).
